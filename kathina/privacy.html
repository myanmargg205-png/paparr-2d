Privacy Policy

App: ကထိန်အလှူမှတ်တမ်း (Kathina Video) · Package: com.paparr.kathina · Effective date: 28 November 2026

Overview

This app is a simple viewer for a family's Kathina donation ceremony videos. It opens a web page listing the videos and plays them through YouTube.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It has:

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone, or location
  • No device permissions requested

Third-party services

When you tap a video, it opens on YouTube (or in the YouTube app), and the video list page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Your use of those services is covered by their own privacy policies:

  • YouTube / Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Children

The app contains family religious ceremony videos suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
